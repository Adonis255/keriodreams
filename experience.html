From the gentle rustle of garden leaves to the aromatic smoke rising from the charcoal jiko, explore what makes visiting Kerio Dreams special.

Nature & Serenity

Lush Garden Dining

Our outdoor garden is an expansive sanctuary of lush green lawns, blooming bougainvillea, and shaded wooden tables. Under the Eldoret sun or relaxing in the afternoon breeze, diners enjoy unhurried meals with family, travelers, and friends.

As dusk settles, ambient lanterns illuminate the paths, creating an enchanting dining setting unlike any other along Kisumu Road.

Rustic Warmth

Rustic Indoor Dining Hall

Inside, our dining hall boasts exposed timber trusses, handcrafted woven pendant chandeliers, and warm wood furnishings. Expansive windows frame sweeping views of the garden grounds, ensuring an open, natural ambiance in any weather.

It is a welcoming venue for business luncheons, evening dinner dates, and guests seeking a quieter dining enclave.

The Jiko Heritage

Authentic Charcoal-Grilling

The heart of authentic Kenyan hospitality is the charcoal jiko. Our grill masters season select prime cuts of goat and chicken simply with sea salt and garlic, roasting them slowly over glowing acacia embers.

The result is crisp, caramelized bark on the outside and rich, succulent meat inside, carved fresh and served piping hot on wooden boards.

Shared Feasts

Family & Group Celebrations

Food in Kenya is meant for sharing. At Kerio Dreams, our long tables accommodate multi-generational families, traveling parties, and milestone anniversaries.

Children enjoy the garden lawns while adults savor leisurely conversations over steaming pots of ginger Dawa tea and grilled platters.
