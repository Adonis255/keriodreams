Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about dining, takeaway, and reservations.

How do I reserve a table in advance?

You can use our interactive Table Booking page to select your preferred date, time slot, party size, and seating preference (Garden, Indoor, Family). Our team confirms your reservation directly via WhatsApp or phone call.

Can I order takeaway in advance while traveling?

Yes! Travelers along Kisumu Road frequently call or use our Online Order page to order hot nyama choma, kuku kienyeji, chips, or traditional meals 30 to 45 minutes ahead for quick pickup.

How can I check current menu prices?

Our full digital menu is available on the Menu page. Please note that prices in this prototype represent sample demonstration data for owner verification before official launch.

Do you offer food delivery in Eldoret?

Delivery inquiries are welcomed. Rider availability and delivery charges depend on distance from our Kisumu Road location and must be confirmed with our team directly.

Where is the restaurant located?

Kerio Dreams is conveniently situated along Kisumu Road, directly opposite Soin Hotel in Eldoret, Kenya. There is ample parking and easy roadside access.
