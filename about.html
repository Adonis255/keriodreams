Our Heritage & Philosophy

Rooted in Kenyan Flavour. Made for Sharing.

Kerio Dreams Restaurant was created to provide a welcoming haven in Eldoret where authentic culinary tradition, generous food, and genuine hospitality meet.

Our Philosophy

The Comfort of Authentic Flavours

In Kenyan culture, mealtime is a celebration of connection. From slow-roasted goat ribs seasoned simply with salt on the jiko to the earthy richness of managu greens sauteed with cream and tomatoes, our kitchen is dedicated to preserving time-honored preparations.

We believe that good food requires patience: charcoal embers burning steadily, freshly harvested ingredients, and dishes made from scratch every single day.

The Setting

A Sanctuary Along Kisumu Road

Conveniently situated opposite Soin Hotel in Eldoret, Kerio Dreams offers an immediate respite from highway traffic and city hustle. Step beyond our gates into peaceful landscaped greenery, towering palms, and the soothing sounds of birdsong.

Whether you are on a cross-country journey between Eldoret and Kisumu or a local resident enjoying a weekend luncheon, our doors are open with open-hearted warmth.

What Sets Kerio Dreams Apart

🔥 Charcoal Jiko Cooking

Real charcoal embers infuse each cut of meat with authentic natural aroma, tenderness, and rich taste.

🌿 Garden Oasis Atmosphere

Spacious, manicured outdoor lawns provide clean fresh air and peaceful relaxation for all guests.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family & Community First

Platters crafted for communal sharing, creating lasting memories across generations.
